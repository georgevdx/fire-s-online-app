    Add people

    Each new email is a new subscription under this company name. You (the owner) pay — inspectors do not. They never Subscribe. Tap Add inspector / manager to open the subscription page. There you type the email, choose Inspector or Manager, and see the fees. One person is one company. Only you (the owner) can Remove someone. That deletes their email and password from the cloud. Then they can Subscribe under another company name.

    How a remote Inspector / Manager joins
    1. You tap Add inspector / manager. That opens the subscription page. Type their email, choose Inspector or Manager, then tap Subscribe this email. You (the owner) pay. They do not pay.
    2. Tell them (they can be anywhere): Open Fire-S → 2. Create password → type that same email → choose a password.
    3. They Login. They must not tap Subscribe. Subscribe is only for a new company owner, or for you adding a person.
    4. They inspect on their own phone. Phone and desktop use that same email.
