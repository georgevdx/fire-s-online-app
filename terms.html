1. What Fire-S is

Fire-S is a paid fire safety inspection app. The owner subscribes for a company. Staff log in with the email the owner added. Fire-S helps you inspect sites, keep photos and GPS, and export a client PDF. It does not replace a qualified inspector, a municipal approval, or a fire-safety certificate issued by an authority.

2. Who may use it

Fire-S is for businesses. It is not for children. The person who taps Subscribe is the Owner for that company and must have authority to bind that business. You must give a real company name and a working email.

3. Price

  • Inspector, Manager, Owner and Viewer pay the same. Role does not change the price.
  • One email is one paid seat. Phone and desktop with the same email are one login, not a second seat.
  • Do not enter the same email twice to try to get a second seat without paying.
  • Annual is billed as 10 months (2 months included).

Prices are in South African Rand. If Company S must charge VAT, that will be shown on the invoice. Company S may change the price with notice to the owner. A price change does not apply to a period already paid.

The app does not take a card yet. After you subscribe, Company S invoices the owner for each paid email. Access to Fire-S may be paused if an invoice stays unpaid.

When a card or instant-EFT payment button is added later (for example PayFast), these terms still apply. The payment screen will show the amount before you confirm. Card details are taken by that payment company, not stored in Fire-S.

5. Starting, changing and cancelling

  • Subscribe creates the owner login and the company.
  • The owner can switch Monthly or Annual under Home → Subscription.
  • Adding a person in Personnel adds another paid email from the next invoice.
  • Removing a person stops that email as a future seat. It does not automatically refund a period already billed.
  • To cancel the whole company, the owner asks Company S through Support in Fire-S.

Until a card button is live, cancellation and refunds are handled on the invoice: write to Company S, and we confirm in writing. After a card button is live, a monthly plan can be stopped before the next month; an annual plan is for the year already paid unless Company S agrees a refund in writing.
