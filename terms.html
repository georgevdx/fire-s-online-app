1. What Fire-S is

Fire-S is a paid fire safety inspection app. The owner subscribes for a company. Staff log in with the email the owner added. Fire-S helps you inspect sites, keep photos and GPS, and export a client PDF. It does not replace a qualified inspector, a municipal approval, or a fire-safety certificate issued by an authority.

2. Who may use it

Fire-S is for businesses. It is not for children. The person who taps Subscribe is the Owner for that company and must have authority to bind that business. You must give a real company name and a working email.

3. Price

  • Inspectors and other staff do not pay. Fire-S invoices the owner, not the inspector.
  • Each new email the owner adds is a new subscription. Phone and desktop with the same email are one login, not a second subscription.
  • Do not enter the same email twice to try to get a second seat without paying.
  • Annual is billed as 10 months (2 months included).

Prices are in South African Rand. Fire-S is not registered for VAT, so no VAT is added. The invoice amount is R250 per month or R2 500 per year, per subscription. Fire-S may change the price with notice to the owner. A price change does not apply to a period already paid.

The app does not take a card yet. After you subscribe, Fire-S invoices the owner for each subscribed email. Inspectors do not receive an invoice. Access to Fire-S may be paused if an invoice stays unpaid.

When a card or instant-EFT payment button is added later (for example PayFast), these terms still apply. The payment screen will show the amount before you confirm. Card details are taken by that payment company, not stored in Fire-S.

5. Starting, changing and cancelling

  • Subscribe creates the owner login and the company.
  • The owner can switch Monthly or Annual under Home → Subscription.
  • An annual subscription renews automatically until the owner cancels it by hand under Home → Subscription.
  • Seven days before the expiry date, Home shows a reminder. The owner can tap Renew to keep the login, or Cancel subscription to stop.
  • Adding a person in Personnel starts a new subscription for that email. Fire-S invoices the owner, not that person.
  • Removing a person stops that email as a future seat. It does not automatically refund a period already billed.
  • To cancel, the owner opens Home → Subscription, taps Cancel subscription, and confirms. Invoices for the next period stop. The current period stays until the expiry date already shown.

Cancelling a subscription does not delete the company name, company details, or inspections. That data stays in the Fire-S cloud. Until a card button is live, refunds are handled on the invoice: write through Support in Fire-S, and we confirm in writing. After a card button is live, a monthly plan can be stopped before the next month; an annual plan is for the year already paid unless Fire-S agrees a refund in writing.
