Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 August 2026

Fire-S is a fire safety inspection app. This page tells you what information we store when you subscribe, log in, inspect a site, or download a report.

Who this is for

Fire-S is for businesses that inspect fire safety. It is not for children. Owners subscribe for their company. Staff log in with the email the owner added.

What we collect

  • Login email and password (password is stored by our sign-in provider, not shown in the app).
  • Company name, address, phone numbers, email and logo for the client PDF.
  • Chosen billing: monthly (R250) or annual (R2 500), per subscription.
  • Inspection details: site name, address, occupancy, answers, comments, photos, GPS pin, dates and reports.
  • Staff names, emails and roles that the owner or manager adds.
  • Support / “report a problem” messages you send from the app.

Why we collect it

  • To create your company subscription and let the right people log in.
  • To complete fire safety inspections and make the client PDF.
  • To show owners and managers company reports and dashboards.
  • To contact you about price, support and the service.

Where it is stored

Inspection and company data is stored in the Fire-S cloud (Supabase) and, on the phone, in the browser until it syncs. Photos you attach sit with that inspection. Google Play only receives the listing information uploaded for Fire-S, not your inspection files.

Who can see it

People in your company, at the role the owner set (Owner, Manager, Inspector, Viewer). Fire-S can access data to run and support the app. We do not sell your inspection data.

Payment

Fire-S is a paid subscription of R250 per month or R2 500 per year, billed per subscription. One email is one subscription. The same person uses that email on phone and desktop. The owner who subscribes, and who adds personnel, pays. Inspectors and other staff do not pay. No VAT is added — Fire-S is not registered for VAT. The app does not take a card yet. Fire-S invoices the owner for each subscribed email. When someone taps Subscribe, or an owner adds a new email in Personnel, that is a new subscription. Fire-S records the company name, the owner to invoice, the new email and the monthly or annual choice so we can invoice the owner.

How long we keep it

We keep company and inspection records while the subscription is active, and for a reasonable period after so the owner can export reports. Ask Fire-S if you need a company or login removed.

Your choices

  • Owners can change company details, people and the subscription package in the app.
  • Use Forgot password on Access if a login needs a reset.
  • Contact Fire-S through Support in the app to ask for a copy or deletion.

Terms

Use of Fire-S is also covered by the Terms and conditions. Fire-S is proprietary software. You may use it; you may not copy it or feed it to an AI to make a look-alike app.

Contact

Fire-S, South Africa. Use Support inside Fire-S, or the contact email on the Google Play listing when that listing is live.
