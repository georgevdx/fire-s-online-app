Full description
Fire-S is the professional fire safety inspection app from Company S.

Subscribe as the company owner, add your inspectors, then work on the phone:

• Find or start an inspection
• Fill the Q&A checklist
• Take photos and capture GPS
• Finish the inspection
• Export the client PDF with your company letterhead

Owners and managers also get Home reports, personnel, company details, and a management dashboard on tablet or PC.

Packages: Field, Operations, Executive and Enterprise. Billing is monthly or annual, per user email. One email is one seat on phone and desktop. Price is confirmed by Company S. The app does not take a card in this version.
