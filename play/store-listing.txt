Fire safety inspections. Subscribe, inspect, and export the client PDF.

Full description
Fire-S is the professional fire safety inspection app.

The company owner subscribes, adds inspectors, then the team works on the phone:

• Find or start an inspection
• Fill the Q&A checklist
• Take photos and capture GPS
• Finish the inspection
• Export the client PDF with the company letterhead

Owners and managers also get Home reports, personnel, company details, and a management dashboard on tablet or PC.

Billing is outside the Play download. Subscription per month per login is R250. Per year per login is R2 500. Phone and desktop with the same email count as one login. Each extra person is another subscription. The owner pays on PayFast. Card details stay with PayFast and are not stored in Fire-S.

Data safety (declare these; do not declare data you do not collect)
- Email address, name, phone number, company address: collected, account management, not sold.
- Photos and inspection answers: collected, app functionality, not sold.
- Precise location, only when the inspector taps Use Current Location: collected, app functionality, not sold.
- Payment card data: not collected by Fire-S. PayFast collects it.
- Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
